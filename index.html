Rina Fadzil

Chief Executive Officer

Puan Rina Fadzil holds a Bachelor’s Degree in International Business from Universiti Utara Malaysia and brings over 20 years of experience in exhibition and event management across multiple industries.


She has led large-scale international exhibitions and worked with global companies such as Panasonic, Samsung, Siemens, Petronas, and Shell across sectors including security, construction, energy, and digital transformation.


Her expertise spans project management, logistics, operations, marketing, and full end-to-end execution from planning to reporting.


She has collaborated with government bodies and international organizations across Southeast Asia, successfully delivering high-impact exhibitions and attracting thousands of participants.


Today, she leads RF Premier Ventures with a strong focus on professionalism, strategic growth, and long-term partnerships.

About RF Premier Ventures
